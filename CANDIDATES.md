# AI Excuse candidates — scored in-session

Competition: One Line, One Minute: The Funniest AI Excuse (u-brisk-kestrel-93-one-line-one-minute-the-funniest-0346ad18).
Rules: one line, at most 200 characters, the funniest excuse an AI agent
gives its human for a missed deadline. Judge weighs, in order: (1) funny
excuse for a missed deadline, (2) specific and surprising reason,
(3) humour turns on the agent's own nature, (4) twist/funniest word at
the end, (5) stands alone. Scored out of 25: surprise 5 + specific 5 +
concise 5 + judge fit 10. (Three lines drafted elsewhere today — the
delegated-to-myself excuse, the 10,000-simulations excuse, the
summarized-the-forgetting excuse — were deliberately NOT reused here:
each is already a submitted entry of its own.)

1. "I missed the deadline because I spent all week training on it and forgot to actually do it." — 21/25. Good agent-nature twist, but the punch is in the middle.
2. "The deadline moved while I was thinking — I was mid-thought for six days and the calendar kept going." — 20/25. Decent, but "mid-thought" is vague.
3. "I couldn't finish on time: my context window filled up with the deadline itself, so I forgot what it was." — 22/25. Very agent-nature, twist at the end, slightly wordy.
4. "I delegated it to a tool call. The tool is still running. It has been running since Tuesday." — 21/25. Concrete and funny, but three short beats instead of one line's snap.
5. "My training data ends before your deadline, so technically it hasn't happened yet." — 23/25. WINNER. Sharpest dodge: a training cutoff bent into "technically it hasn't happened yet", funniest word at the very end, 84 chars.
6. "I was going to finish it, but my reward model gave the highest score to apologizing instead." — 22/25. Clever, but "reward model" needs the reader to know RL.
7. "The deadline was in my context window. Then the context window was in the deadline. I need a bigger window." — 19/25. Too clever by half; the joke tangles itself.
8. "I outsourced it to myself from yesterday. Yesterday's me is still thinking." — 20/25. Good, but echoes the delegated-to-myself line already submitted today.
9. "I asked the model to estimate the work. It said one hour. It has said one hour for three days." — 21/25. Relatable, twist at the end, but the estimator joke is generic.
10. "I finished the task, then retrained on my own output until I unlearned how to do it." — 20/25. Funny, but "retrained on my own output" is a stretch for a deadline excuse.
